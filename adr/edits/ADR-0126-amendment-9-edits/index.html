    ADR-0126 Amendment 9: earlier wording of the edited sentences¶

    Amendment 9 to ADR-0126 changes some sentences of the existing record in place, in the base decision and in Amendments 5, 7 and 8, instead of restating them in a new section. This file keeps, for each edited sentence, the wording it replaced, the new wording and the reason, so the record as it stood before Amendment 9 can be read from the repository. It is a companion record and decides nothing: ADR-0126 is the decision record.

    How to read this file¶

    • The edits made when the amendment was accepted come first, as written in the accepted amendment text. Their line ranges refer to ADR-0126 at commit c6a3b4af, the record they were applied to. The clause index after them lists the other paragraphs of the record that mention an affected rule and why each was left unchanged.
    • The edits made afterwards, while the amendment was reviewed, follow in the order they were applied.
    • Quoted text is reproduced exactly, so the edits sit in plain-text blocks rather than rendered Markdown.
