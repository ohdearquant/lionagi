### Changed

- The `li studio` launcher now lives in `lionhub.cli.studio` and the `li schedule` client in `lionhub.cli.schedule`; the
  command catalog selects them there, and `li schedule` no longer imports `lionagi.studio.cli`. Through 1.x,
  `lionagi.studio.cli` still exports every name it exported before as the same object. A patch on a
  `lionagi.studio.cli` attribute no longer reaches the moved code, which looks its names up in its new module: patch
  `lionhub.cli.studio` or `lionhub.cli.schedule` instead.
