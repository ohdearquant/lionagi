### Changed

- `DataLogger` persists only when it is given a directory. `DataLoggerConfig.persist_dir` now defaults to `None` and `auto_save_on_exit` to `False`, and the settings `LOG_PERSIST_DIR` and `LOG_AUTO_SAVE_ON_EXIT` default to `None` and `False`. Using lionagi as a library no longer writes `data/logs` into the working directory. To keep the old behaviour, set `LOG_PERSIST_DIR` (and `LOG_AUTO_SAVE_ON_EXIT=true` for the save at exit), or pass `persist_dir` to the logger's config.
- Without a directory, a logger at capacity drops its oldest entries instead of writing them out.
- `DataLogger.dump()` and `adump()` with no directory and no `persist_path` raise `ValueError`, and keep the entries.
- The global hook logger no longer writes to `./data/logs` by default, and `Branch.__aexit__` dumps its logs only when its logger has a directory.
