### Added

- A routed Anthropic request that the provider rejects with a 4xx other than 401, 403 or 429
  now records two names on its attempt in the credential ledger and on the routed error: the
  provider error type and a rejection class, each from a closed set. A refusal can be told
  apart without keeping any of the provider's message. A body over 64 KiB, or one that does not
  parse, is recorded as `other`. This covers the non-streamed attempt.
