### Added

- A Studio approval records who granted or denied it. When the grant or deny request carries a
  verified `X-Studio-Decision-Token` (the decision capability `li studio` pairs its page with),
  the approval and its evidence row record the principal's id, epoch, method, origin and scope,
  as Operator decisions already do, and `GET /api/approvals/{id}` returns them. A request without
  one is still accepted and recorded unattributed. The evidence hash includes the principal only
  when one is recorded, so existing evidence chains still verify. Existing stores gain the columns
  as NULL on their next writable open.
- An older lionagi checking the evidence chain of a store this version wrote reports a content
  hash mismatch on every grant or deny that recorded a principal, because it does not know the
  field. The rows are intact; check the chain with this version or later.
