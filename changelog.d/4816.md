### Fixed

- A streamed settlement in the credential ledger could, after a credit, leave an account's settled spend above the signed 64-bit maximum; the account then read as `ledger_unavailable` for the rest of the month. That settlement is now refused with `budget_exhausted` and the attempt stays open.

### Changed

- `CredentialLedger.adjust()` refuses an adjustment that would take the account's month past the signed 64-bit range with `AccountRoutingError(budget_exhausted)`, the same refusal every other ledger write gives, instead of `ValueError`. An adjustment that would make settled spend negative still raises `ValueError`.
