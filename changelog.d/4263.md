### Changed

- Codex launches started by `li agent`, orchestration planners, and orchestration workers now turn off the signed-in account's apps and plugins by default, including launches without a role profile. To opt out, launch through an agent profile that sets `contain_account_integrations: false`; naming `account_connectors` continues to grant access for that launch. A branch cloned from the orchestrator (a spawned flow node, fanout synthesis) does not carry its connectors; it is contained unless the orchestrator opted out with `contain_account_integrations: false` and named no connectors, in which case the clone keeps that opt-out.
